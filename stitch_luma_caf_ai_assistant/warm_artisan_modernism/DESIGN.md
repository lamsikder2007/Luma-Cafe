## Brand & Style

This design system expresses the sensory intimacy of an artisan specialty café balanced with the quiet poise of high-end editorial minimalism. The visual environment evokes morning sunlight filtered through linen, the tactile warmth of handcrafted ceramic mugs, and the quiet precision of third-wave brewing.

The emotional target is unhurried luxury: serene, welcoming, and grounded. Interactions feel intentional and hospitable rather than rushed or transactional. 

Visual style foundation:
- **Warm Minimalist Tactility:** Heavy natural breathing room, soft structural edges, and a rejection of stark digital whites or sterile cold greys.
- **Editorial Subtlety:** High-contrast, literary display typography balanced by crisp, humanist geometric utility.
- **Organic Harmony:** Deep roasty earth tones rooted by soothing botanicals and gilded warmth, echoing raw linen, polished walnut, unglazed clay, and aged bronze.

## Colors

The palette is derived directly from raw café materials: roasted coffee beans, botanical indoor plantings, spun caramel crema, and hand-milled ceramics.

### Functional Application

- **Surface Base (`#FAF7F2`):** The primary canvas background. Replaces pure digital white across the entire viewport to eliminate eye strain and cultivate immediate tactile warmth.
- **Surface Raised / Cards (`#FFFDF9`):** Pure light cream used for cards, popovers, and elevated modal panels, providing soft, luminous contrast against `#FAF7F2`.
- **Surface Sunk / Recessed (`#F5EFE6`):** Secondary container surface for inset controls, input fields, and pill backgrounds.
- **Primary Espresso (`#2C1D11` & `#3D2B1F`):** Deep, velvety roast used for core actions, structural headers, high-emphasis text, and grounding anchor states.
- **Secondary Olive (`#3E4F3E` & `#2A382A`):** Earthy herbal green applied to conscious highlights, botanical accents, sustainability badges, active states, and seasonal features.
- **Tertiary Caramel / Gold (`#C68B59` & `#D99B66`):** Warm amber used for micro-interactions, star ratings, specialty item tags, focus halos, and secondary interactive moments.
- **Muted Terracotta Accent (`#B85D43`):** Reserved strictly for gentle alerts, error boundaries, or urgent ordering notices without disrupting the organic tone.

## Typography

Typography establishes an editorial tension between literary, old-world craft and effortless modern digital clarity.

- **Headlines & Display (Playfair Display):** Conveys bespoke craftsmanship, warmth, and culinary elegance. Headlines should prioritize natural title-casing or sentence-casing; avoid harsh full capitalization.
- **Body & Controls (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Selected for its friendly geometry, soft curves, and superior digital legibility at small scale. It harmonizes seamlessly with the organic curves of the surfaces.
- **Numerics & Currencies:** In menu contexts and pricing tables, align prices using tabular figures in `Plus Jakarta Sans` semi-bold to ensure visual balance alongside editorial product titles.

## Layout & Spacing

This design system uses a relaxed 12-column responsive fluid grid anchored by a maximum content width of `1200px` to maintain focused visual cohesion.

- **Desktop (>= 1024px):** 12 columns, `32px` gutters, fluid container with minimum `48px` outer margins. Generous section padding (`space-3xl` to `space-4xl`) provides breathable negative space.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8 columns, `24px` gutters, `32px` outer margins. Menu cards transition to a 2-column format.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns, `16px` gutters, `16px` to `20px` screen edges. Section vertical spacing condenses to `space-2xl` to respect screen real estate while maintaining an open, unhurried cadence.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy relies on ambient warmth and physical layering rather than dramatic artificial drop shadows.

- **Ambient Shading:** Shadows are ultra-diffuse and tinted with the warm undertones of roasted espresso (`rgba(44, 29, 17, 0.04)` to `rgba(44, 29, 17, 0.08)`), preventing the cold greyness of default shadow models.
- **Level 1 (Default Cards):** `0px 4px 20px -2px rgba(44, 29, 17, 0.04)`. Barely perceptible separation of `#FFFDF9` above `#FAF7F2`.
- **Level 2 (Hover / Elevated Panels):** `0px 12px 32px -4px rgba(44, 29, 17, 0.07)`. Used for interactive card states, dropdown menus, and cart drawers.
- **Level 3 (Modals / Overlays):** `0px 24px 48px -8px rgba(44, 29, 17, 0.12)`. Coupled with a warm backdrop blur: `backdrop-filter: blur(8px); background-color: rgba(44, 29, 17, 0.25)`.
- **Border Integration:** Elevated surfaces utilize a 1px border colored `rgba(44, 29, 17, 0.05)` to softly delineate cards when positioned over similar warm cream grounds.

## Shapes

The design system embraces generous, soft geometry reminiscent of pebble-smooth ceramics and organic forms.

- **Cards & Primary Modules:** Standard cards leverage `rounded-2xl` (1.5rem / 24px), while feature showcases and hero containers utilize `rounded-3xl` (2rem / 32px).
- **Interactive Triggers (Buttons & Tags):** Pure pill shapes (`rounded-full` / 9999px) for primary actions, category filters, and status chips to preserve a soft, inviting touch target.
- **Form Controls:** Text inputs and selectors use `1rem` (16px) corner rounding to maintain structural continuity with the card system.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Filled in Espresso (`#2C1D11`) with text in `#FFFDF9`. Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), `h-12` or `h-14`, px-8. Subtle scale down (`scale-98`) and gentle deepening of shade on hover (`#3D2B1F`).
- **Secondary:** Filled in Olive (`#3E4F3E`) with text in `#FAF7F2`. Pill-shaped, used for sustainable, plant-based, or artisan storytelling callouts.
- **Soft / Ghost:** Background in `#F5EFE6`, text in Espresso (`#2C1D11`). Hover brings a gentle wash of Caramel tint (`#C68B59` at 10% opacity).

### Chips & Filter Pills
- **Category Filter:** Pill-shaped, `h-9`, `px-4`, font-size `13px`. Default is unselected with `#FFFDF9` surface and `1px solid rgba(44, 29, 17, 0.08)`. Selected state transforms to solid `#2C1D11` with white text.
- **Status Badges:** Small (`rounded-full`, `px-3`, `py-1`, `text-xs`). Olive background (`#3E4F3E` at 12% opacity) for "Single Origin" / "Organic"; Caramel background (`#C68B59` at 15% opacity) for "House Roast" / "Seasonal".

### Cards
- **Product & Menu Cards:** Elevated surface `#FFFDF9`, `rounded-2xl`, Level 1 ambient espresso shadow. Image containers inside use matching nested border radii (`rounded-xl`). Padding is generous (`p-6`). Content includes Playfair Display item title, tasting notes in muted body, and tabular price.
- **Feature / Heritage Card:** Large `rounded-3xl` container, styled with warm Sand/Olive tinted background (`#F5EFE6`), housing evocative photography and artisan brand narratives.

### Form Inputs
- **Text Fields:** Surface `#F5EFE6`, border-free or bordered with `1px solid transparent`. Height `48px`, `rounded-xl`, `px-4`. Focus state replaces border with `1px solid #C68B59` accompanied by a soft caramel glow ring (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 3px rgba(198, 139, 89, 0.2)`).
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Curved (`rounded-md` for checkboxes, `rounded-full` for radios). Unchecked state uses `#EADFCF` border. Checked state fills with `#2C1D11` displaying an off-white icon.

### Specialized Café Components
- **Roast & Flavor Profile Scale:** A segmented dot-or-bar indicator showing acidity, body, and roast level using rich caramel (`#C68B59`) and cream inactive tones.
- **Tasting Note Tags:** Small, lightweight text labels encased in low-contrast beige containers (`#FAF7F2`) with subtle borders to outline flavor notes (e.g., "Bergamot", "Stone Fruit", "Dark Cacao").
