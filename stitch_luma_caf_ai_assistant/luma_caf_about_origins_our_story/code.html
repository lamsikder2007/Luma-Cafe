The Dhaka Genesis

Cultivating Calm Amidst the City's Relentless Rhythm

Luma was born from an intimate desire: to carve out unhurried spaces in the vibrant density of Dhaka. We saw a metropolis rich in literary history and tea addas, yet yearning for a contemporary coffee culture rooted in world-class craft and slow living.

Rather than industrializing caffeine as fuel, we re-anchored it as an intentional pause. Every detail—from our custom acoustic plaster arches that absorb urban noise, to our ceramic vessels crafted by regional potters in Rayer Bazar—was designed to honor the quiet contemplation of the morning cup.

“We do not rush the bean, and we do not rush the guest. In a world moving at breakneck speed, Luma is an invitation to pause, breathe, and savor.”

RS

Rayan Siddiqui

Small-Batch Science

Precision Roasting for Terroir Brilliance

We follow a Nordic-inspired light-to-medium roasting curve. By utilizing gentle conductive heat transfer in our cast iron drum, we highlight the bean's genetic sugar profile while preserving the subtle terroir florals that mass-market roasters incinerate into dark bitterness.
